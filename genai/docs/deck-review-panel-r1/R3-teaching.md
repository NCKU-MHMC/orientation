# R3 — Practical teaching and slide-craft review of `deck-blueprint.md`

Reviewer remit: will this deck teach new lab members whose only background is an intro deep-learning course? Checked against `Generative_Models_Intro_Two_Session_Outline_EN.md` (audience, "Teaching discipline", timing appendix) and the blueprint's own constraint list. Page numbers are blueprint page numbers (L1 = lecture-01, L2 = lecture-02).

## Findings

1. **MUST — The outline's top-priority demo is missing and five unlisted demos were invented instead.** (Blueprint "Demo 對應" table; outline "Suggested new LLM-native demos".) The outline's #1 new demo is the token probability browser for Session 1 ②③, explicitly "to show that the coefficient is a coordinate along the spectrum and that the logprob interface really exists". The blueprint drops it and instead invents `guidance-playground`, `asr-noisy-channel`, `mle-vs-ddo-gradient`, `interface-contract`, `exposure-bias-track` — none of which show students a real token-level logprob, and none of which exist yet (only three 2D demos are built). For an audience that has never seen a logit, the token browser is the single strongest anchor in Session 1. Fix: make the token probability browser the L1 ②③ demo (it can absorb `guidance-playground`'s sliders: temperature, top-p, CFG weight on one token distribution), and cut or downgrade `asr-noisy-channel` and `interface-contract` to static figures.

2. **MUST — L1 pages 9–17 are nine consecutive mathematics pages with no LLM anchor, violating the outline's two-minute anchoring rule.** The outline's Teaching discipline says every divergence property must connect "within two minutes to a concrete phenomenon the students have already seen", LLM-native. In the blueprint, from the KL definition (p9) to the discriminator reading (p17), roughly 20 minutes pass with no phenomenon at all; the first behavioural language appears only in the p18 summary table. Fix: add a one-line anchor on p10 (mode-covering → base model hedging, generic comfort replies in the emotional-support project) and on p11 (mode-seeking → aligned model uniformity, RLHF diversity loss). These are exactly the two ends of the thesis stated on p2, so the anchors also start paying off the opening claim instead of leaving it suspended for 36 minutes.

3. **MUST — L2 p34 packs four jobs onto one slide inside the tightest section of the course.** The page holds a six-node improvement timeline (DCGAN→…→BigGAN plus the distillation-role shift), the DDO prototype claim ($d^*=p/(p+q)$ as the origin of "build a discriminator from your own logprob"), the one-way-transfer claim, and the applications list. The GAN section has 13 min, of which the demo takes 5, leaving 8 min for five content pages; this page alone needs 4. Fix: split into (a) improvement history + applications (Timeline page), (b) move the DDO prototype and one-way-transfer material onto p32, which already states the pointwise-limit isomorphism with the reward model — the two claims belong to the same argument.

4. **MUST — Session 1 demo time is not budgeted anywhere, and the outline's Session 1 minutes assumed zero demos.** The outline's demo plan places all three existing demos in Session 2 and gives them explicit minutes (5/3/3); the blueprint inserts four demos into Session 1 (p13, p24, p35, p44) with no minute figures, into sections whose 36/22/20/32 allocations are already fully spent in the outline (e.g. ③ is 20 min for 9 pages of four-layer content before the ASR demo is added). A demo is never free: load, orient, interact, conclude is 3–4 min minimum, so this is ~14 unbudgeted minutes. Fix: add a minutes column to the "Demo 對應" table, and offset each demo by an explicit cut (see findings 5 and 12 for two candidate cuts).

5. **SHOULD — L1 p12 and p13 teach the same content twice.** p12 is the static B-1 figure (unimodal $q$ fits bimodal $p$ under three divergences); p13 is `divergence-2d-interactive`, which shows exactly that. Merge them: conduct the B-1 discussion inside the interactive demo (or use the demo's screenshot as the slide and go live from it). Saves one page and ~3 min in the section that finding 4 shows is overcommitted.

6. **SHOULD — L1 p17 is three results on one derivation page, and one of them exceeds the stated prerequisites.** $D^*=p/(p+q)$, the objective value $2\,\mathrm{JSD}-2\log 2$, and the mutual-information reading $I(X;Z)$ are each a step; mutual information appears nowhere in Appendix A's pre-course self-check, so intro-DL students meet the symbol cold. Fix: keep $D^*$ and the objective value on the slide with the plain-language classifier reading ("JSD is how well an optimal classifier can tell the two sources apart" — this is what DDO on p41 actually reuses); move the $I(X;Z)$ formalism to speaker notes or drop it.

7. **SHOULD — L1 p40 carries three separate claims with no figure.** (β governs safety and diversity jointly with the Kirk et al. citation; the structural limit of a pointwise scorer; LLM-as-judge shares the limit.) Split into two pages: (a) the β consequence, anchored to the emotional-support system (that anchor is in the outline and is the section's best one); (b) the pointwise-scorer limit + LLM-as-judge. Page (b) is also the page L2 p32 later mirrors, so it deserves its own visual.

8. **SHOULD — L1 p45 mixes the spectrum-position argument with the DPO comparison table.** "DDO acts at both ends" is the payoff of the whole session and needs the SpectrumRows visual to itself; the three-row DPO/DDO table is a different comparison. Split, or move the DPO table to p46 where the $\beta$ parameterisation is already being discussed.

9. **SHOULD — L1 p47 crams a results table plus two interpretive observations onto one slide.** The two observations (MLE retraining degrades the model → forward KL ceiling; top-k/top-p was masking distribution defects) are each an argument step the outline treats as load-bearing for the session's thesis. Split: numbers page, then observations page. As one page it will either be illegible or read as a wall of text at minute 115.

10. **SHOULD — Five pages are described as 回接/回顧 and will tempt the writer into visible meta-commentary.** L1 p39 ("表回接"), L2 p2 ("ContractCard 回顧"), L2 p34 ("DDO 回接"), L2 p43 ("回到課程主張…如今三列與家族全部就位"), L2 p44 ("LayerStack 回接"). Blueprint rule 1 bans exactly the prose these labels invite ("回顧上一堂…"、"還記得…"). Fix: for each, the expanded slide states the fact, never the act of returning to it. Examples: p39 → 「reward model 是 $p_{\text{data}}$.logprob 的代理,介面需求表中唯一不可得的一格由它補上」; L2 p43 → replace 「如今三列與家族全部就位」 (course-progress narration) with the substantive claim: 「含糊迴避出自 forward KL 訓練,千篇一律出自 reverse KL 對齊;訓練目標、解碼設定、權重微調三列各自對應一個可移動的位置」. Add this phrasing rule to the blueprint's writing spec so it covers all five pages at once.

11. **SHOULD — GAN section arithmetic does not close.** 13 min − 5 min demo = 8 min for pages 29–32 and 34, and p31 is itself dense (loss table + the Arjovsky decomposition + per-loss pathology commentary). Even after finding 3's split, p31 needs trimming: keep the two-column minimax/non-saturating table and the one-sentence reading of each term on the slide; the derivation of $\mathrm{KL}(p_g\|p_{\text{data}})-2\,\mathrm{JSD}$ goes to speaker notes with the citation. Students at this level need the conclusion (which loss buys which pathology), not the algebra, in 90 seconds.

12. **SHOULD — L1 p29 is overloaded for a 20-min section that averages ~2 min/page.** ICL-as-implicit-Bayes formula + Xie citation + memory-agent reading + lost-in-the-middle + Liu citation on one slide. Fix without adding a page: keep the formula and the memory-agent sentence ("memory 是選擇進入後驗的證據") on p29; move lost-in-the-middle to p30, where it is the natural failure-mode row next to RAG's and fine-tuning's failure modes.

13. **SHOULD — L1 p3 shows a spectrum-position column before the spectrum exists.** At minute ~3 the mapping table's third column ("光譜位置") refers to a construct defined 30+ minutes later; for this audience it is noise on the motivating slide. Fix: p3 shows topic + probabilistic-question columns only; the full three-column table appears at L2 p42, where the outline places the replay and where students can actually read the third column.

14. **CONSIDER — L2 p7's `interface-contract` demo has no stated behaviour.** Every other demo's purpose is one clause; this one is a name. A "demo" of a two-method contract is likely a static diagram wearing an iframe. Either specify what a student manipulates and what changes on screen, or replace with the ContractCard component and return the ~3 min to section ②.

15. **CONSIDER — Session 2's opening is a restatement, not a motivation.** L1 p49 ends with the open question 「一個不提供 logprob 的模型要如何訓練?」; L2 p2 opens with a neutral problem setting and the question is answered only implicitly at p4. Open L2 with that question as the first content slide (it is subject matter, not meta-commentary, and it is the outline's own designed transition), then let p3's table answer it. Cost: zero pages; the existing p2 text becomes the second half of the slide.

## Verdict on remit items 5 (openings/closings)

Session 1 opens well (thesis p2 + lab table p3, modulo finding 13) and closes actionably (homework p49, correctly phrased as an open question rather than a "next session" preview). Session 2 closes actionably (p44: layers 2–4 need only logprob, no extra training resources) but opens flat (finding 15).

**Totals: 4 MUST, 9 SHOULD, 2 CONSIDER.**
